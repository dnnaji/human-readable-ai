## Transcript

Both sequences start at one. The only difference is the rule we apply at each step.

The first sequence adds one. The second doubles. After three steps, their values are four and eight.

After six steps, adding one gives seven. Doubling gives sixty-four. The doubling rule acts on the new value each time.

After n steps, the two formulas are one plus n and two to the power n. The starting value is one, and n is a whole number of steps.
